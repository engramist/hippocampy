## What we learned

1. **The only gain came from the prompt.** Telling the model to cite a line or
   abstain fixed exactly the "right turn in the bundle, answered from another"
   cases (DMR valid_29/55/63/70).
2. **Answer-model size is not the limit.** gemma4:26b scored no better, ignored
   the abstain instruction, and was 4× slower (R40).
3. **Better retrieval did not become better answers.** The reranker raised
   LoCoMo-10 evidence recall at 6 turns from 0.33 to 0.47 offline and still lost
   on accuracy (see `2026-10-m2-reranker.md`).
4. **Presentation noise is large.** Reordering turns that share a timestamp
   changed 45/50 DMR prompts and flipped 6 answers (R42). Any change that
   reshuffles evidence moves about ±5 of ~110 questions on its own, so the
   gates at this size cannot see effects under about 5 points. The repeated
   "gained 1–3, lost 3–8" pattern is consistent with that noise plus a dev set
   main has been implicitly tuned on.
5. **Collapse premise was wrong.** R26's "near-duplicates" were same-topic
   turns; max same-speaker same-session cosine was 0.69 (median 0.34).
6. **Measure the measurer.** Two harness bugs cost runs: a 256-token judge cap
   made the reasoning judge reply empty (4/26 verdicts lost on identical
   answers), and an unquoted heredoc emptied a chain's variables. Gates now
   print a "judge problems" count, and chains are checked with `zsh -n` and
   a grep for literal `$PY` before launch.

## What changed in the plan

- Larger dev golden sets (R44: DMR questions 100–299; LoCoMo-10 conv-41/42/43,
  all questions, each its own store) so gates have the power to see 3–5 point
  effects. Held-out sets stay DMR 50–99 and LoCoMo-10 conv-30 (R30).
- Retrieval-ordering tweaks are paused until the large sets exist. The next
  lever is M3 (observations), measured by R35/R36.
