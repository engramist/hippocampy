# B557: the deprecated tracker's gist steps take most of the ingest time

**Status:** Open. Found in the B543 design screen (2026-11-06).

**Principles:** P7, P1 (gates are run on fresh ingests; ingest time bounds how many gates run).

**What was seen.** On R105 (fresh ingest of five LoCoMo conversations), Loop steps 2-3 (whole-message embedding against the gist centroids, schema.org routing) took 301 of the 314 minutes the Loop worker ran. `GistExample` rows are 43% of the rows in `vectors.db`. Under the principled profile these steps only feed the deprecated tracker (B543), which adds Decision / Constraint / Lesson / Procedure records and gates nothing.

**To do (design first).** Measure what the steps cost and what reads their output under the profile. Then decide whether they run lazily, only for the personal developer store, or behind their own flag, and whether GistExample growth is bounded. A benchmark home with the profile on should not pay for the tracker.
