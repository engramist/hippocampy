# B559: the concept walk is seeded with the question's subject and fills its cap with that person's newest turns

**Status:** Implemented. Runs only under the `principled` profile (`graph_linking_limit` 12; main's default is 0, so the walk is off there), so it needs no new flag. Gated in the whole principled-profile gate (B545). Found in the offline profile check (2026-11-06).

**Principles:** P2 (nothing is counted in Python; the walk's query is unchanged), P8 (the subject's facts hang off the subject edge and arrive through the vote and the Observation stage; the walk explores the other Concepts the question names).

**What was seen.** Offline replay of the principled profile on a copy of the R105 store (backfilled; 300 questions, LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50, categories 1-4):
- The walk fired on 299 of 300 questions, always 12 items, 590 tokens on average (p95 708), +943 prompt tokens per question (+46%).
- Of 3,588 walk items, 2 held a gold evidence turn. Recall moved on 2 questions (+2 / -0 over no walk).
- Cause: `analysis.concept_ids` (B547) includes the question's person, so the walk follows the speaker's name. The B470 common-term test in `GraphGateway._bundle_concept_walk` drops a Concept linked to more than 20% of the *store's* Messages; in a store holding five conversations no speaker gets there (John 13.5%, Maria 11.9%, Caroline 8.6%, Deborah 4.1%). The cap then takes "hop 1 first, newest first": the 12 newest turns that mention the person ("What did Caroline research?" got 11 turns of 2023-10-22 that say "Caroline").

**Rule.** The walk starts from the Concepts the question names other than its subject: `QuestionAnalysis.walk_seed_ids()` returns the concept ids less the subject, the anchors (a tie's group) and every Concept with role `person`. No seed left: the walk adds nothing. The walk's own query (`thalamus.bundle_concept_walk`) and the lexical stage's B470 test are unchanged. Without a question analysis (`question_analysis` off) the seeds come from the old resolvers as before.

**Measurement** (replay on the backfilled R105 copy; recall = the harness's text-level evidence recall over every bundle section; gained/lost per question):

Variant 2, a per-conversation common-term test (a seed linked to more than 20% of one conversation's Messages is dropped), was built as a NamedQuery and measured, then removed: it adds no recall over 1 (0.6195 both ways) and saves about 6 tokens per walk, but it needs a definition of "conversation" (sessions grouped by their first-named speaker) that fits LoCoMo's layout, and on a personal store whose sessions have no speaker it collapses to the per-store test (a P4 risk for no measured benefit). Per category (1): cat 1 0.4340, cat 2 0.7976, cat 3 0.3620, cat 4 0.7688. No exception and no logged warning in any arm. The walk is still token-heavy for its yield (9 gold items in 2,447); the profile gate judges it.

Flag off / profile main: the walk limit is 0, `_stage_concept_walk` returns before reading seeds; replay of profile main on the un-backfilled copy is byte-identical to the replay before this change, 300 of 300 prompts.

**Tests.** `tests/test_b559_walk_seeds.py`: the subject, anchors and persons are not seeds; the walk adds nothing when only the subject is named (the walk query never runs); a named entity is still walked and the subject's own turns are not; profile main runs no walk. `tests/test_b547_question_analysis.py` updated: the analysis' walk seeds exclude the subject.
