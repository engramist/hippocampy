# B569: tie a relation node to the named person when the turn says who it is

**Status:** Open (follow-up of item 3, the group and relation subjects; filed 2026-11-14 at the review session's
request). Not done in item 3's PR, which keeps the two nodes apart.

**Principles:** P8 (subjects are the backbone), P3 (a rule decides; the model only proposes).

**What.** With `[observations] group_subjects` on, "my son" is the Concept `rel:<speaker>:son` (RELATION_OF the
speaker), and "Kai" is a named PERSON Concept. When a turn states both together ("my son Kai", "Kai, my son"), they
are two nodes, and Kai's facts and "my son"'s facts are not joined. A question about either one misses the other half.

**Graph edge.** One new edge, `<relation Concept> campy:RELATION_IS <named Concept>`: the relation node is the
named person. At most one per relation node. Written only by rule. Read by the subject queries (a fact of either
node counts for both through this edge).

**Design sketch (for review before code).**
- The construction is the appositive or the predicate nominal, in the dependency parse of one turn:
  - "my son Kai": `appos` or `compound` between a relation noun under the person roots and a PERSON entity the turn
    MENTIONS;
  - "Kai, my son": `appos`;
  - "Kai is my son": copula with "my" + a relation noun.
- The rule writes RELATION_IS from the speaker's relation node to that Concept, when exactly one PERSON entity is in
  the construction.
- Never by guess:
  - not from co-occurrence in a session;
  - not from another speaker's words ("her son Kai" ties nothing);
  - not when two names compete.
- A conflicting second statement (the same relation node tied to another name) is recorded and flagged, never
  overwritten. Two sons are two relation nodes only if the speaker names them apart, which needs its own design;
  until then the first tie wins.

**Measure.** Count the appositive constructions on the five stores before building (how many relation nodes it
would tie). Then measure query hits on conv-26 against the item 3 baseline, with its own prediction and kill line.
