# B533: measure how stable the extractor's predicate choice is

**Status:** Open

Lesson from the first project: a class boundary that two strong models with full context cannot agree on is too fine for a record class. Keep it as a recorded attribute instead. Our extractor chooses one of nine predicates (`did, has_attribute, is_a, located_in, owns, relates_to, prefers, plans, changed_to`). On real stores it filed Andrew's dogs under `prefers`, `relates_to` and `has_attribute`, and the B523 rule had to decide ownership from grammar.

**Work:** run extraction twice (and with a second model if available) over the same user turns on a store copy. Report per-predicate agreement and the confusion matrix. Predicates with low agreement become candidates for "derive by rule" (as B523 did for `owns`) or for merging. This is a measurement card; any change it suggests is its own lever with its own gate.
