# B520: a gate must hold enough of its lever's target questions

**Status:** Done

B505 (count/sum answers) was gated on LoCoMo-10 conv-30 and conv-44, 60 category 1-4 questions each.
Only 4 of those 120 questions were counting questions, so the gate could not move whatever the lever
did. Separately, every lever since mid-October 2026 has been gated on the same two conversations, so
they are reused adaptively and are no longer held out (PRINCIPLES.md 4).

1. `scripts/gate_slice.py`: for a named construction (`count`, `temporal`, `latest`, `list`, `all`;
   a small dict at the top, evaluation routing only, never read by answering code) it counts the
   category 1-4 questions per conversation using the benchmark runner's own dataset loader, checks an
   explicit slice (`--conversations`, `--max-questions`), and proposes a slice with at least
   `--min-targets` (default 10) targets, preferring conversations not in the gate history. It prints
   the exact runner flags. `--record` appends the proposal to the history.
2. `benchmarks/gate_history.json`: conversations used by past gates, seeded with conv-30 and conv-44.
3. `.github/scripts/check_pr_score_effect.py`: a Score effect section with a gate table needs
   `Targets in gate: N` (N >= 10), `all`, or `none — <reason>`. `None — <reason>` sections are
   unchanged. The PR template hint carries the line.

Findings on the current slice (conv-30 + conv-44, 60 per conversation): `count` 4 targets,
`latest` 1, `list` 8, `temporal` 39. `latest` cannot reach 10 on any LoCoMo-10 slice at a 60 cap
(at most 2 across the best pair), so a lever for it needs a different suite.

Serves Principles 1 (the gate must be able to move), 4 (held-out conversations) and 6 (measure
correctly before believing). Evaluation tooling, not a retrieval lever.
