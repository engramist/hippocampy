### Problem & Architectural Delta

While the compression plumbing exists, two critical gaps remain in the emit path:
1. **Lack of Protected Lane Routing:** All sections currently pass through `ContentRouter` without distinguishing invariant ground truths from lossy-tolerant narratives. Critical architectural decisions, negative controls, and active constraints risk subtle semantic dilution during prose/bundle compression.
2. **Always-On vs. Budget-Gated Compression (Pressure-Relief Valve):** `ask.py` currently runs compression in "ALWAYS-ON (Option B)" mode regardless of bundle size. Compressing a small, 400-token bundle adds unnecessary LLM latency (~1-2s), token cost, and risk of information loss. Compression should act as an automatic **pressure-relief valve**, firing only when the assembled bundle exceeds the caller's configured `budget_tokens` (or default budget).

### What it does (scope of delta)

1. **Two-Lane Retrieval Routing (`campy/brain/thalamus/compression/` & `ask.py`):**
   - **Protected Lane (Zero Loss):** Ground-truth architectural decisions, active constraints, and negative controls bypass compression entirely. They are emitted verbatim to guarantee 100% factual accuracy and constraint adherence.
   - **Bulk Lane (Lossy-Tolerant Compression):** Concept narratives, historical summaries, and code extracts are routed to the compressor *only when the assembled bundle exceeds the configured token budget*.
   - **Graph Guardrail (`anti-patterns.md`, `performance-and-debugging.md`):** Filter early, expand late. The retrieval phase prior to compression anchors on top-$k$ entry points from vector search ($k \le 5$), immediately excludes `archived = true` and `[DEPRECATED_BY]` nodes at root, and bounds neighborhood expansion to a maximum traversal depth of 2 hops with a per-node fan-out ceiling of 15 edges to prevent cartesian explosion in the Thalamus.
   - **Maintain Standing Guardrail:** Uphold the rule in `compression/__init__.py`: "Do NOT replace GraphBundleCompressor with a generic JSON compressor." Semantic pruning based on graph signals (`pathway_strength x query similarity`) is strictly preserved.

2. **Budget-Gated Pressure-Relief Valve:**
   - Modify `run_ask()` in `campy/brain/thalamus/ask.py`: Calculate total estimated tokens for the assembled `ContextBundle`.
   - If `total_tokens <= token_budget`: emit bundle directly, skipping the compression stage.
   - If `total_tokens > token_budget`: pass Bulk Lane sections through `ContentRouter` to compress under budget while leaving Protected Lane sections untouched.

3. **Prompt Hardening in `LLMProseCompressor` (`llm_prose.py`):**
   - Refine the prose compression system prompt to explicitly enforce: "Preserve every entity name, decision identifier, requirement, numeric parameter, and negation verbatim; eliminate only conversational filler, restatements, and syntactic redundancy."
