# B453 - Daemon Cold Start Takes 4-7 Minutes (Store Open + Gist-Centroid Recompute Every Start)

State: ready
Priority: P2

Observed 2026-09-23: opening the 7.7 GB RocksDB store took ~150s; and every
start logs "Bootstrapping gist class centroids from seed examples" and
recomputes 7 classes x ~45 seed embeddings (~35 s/class, ~3 min total) even
though the seeds do not change. During any restart the MCP adapter answers
"offline" — which, before B450, silently corrupted benchmark runs. Suggested:
persist/cache centroids keyed by (seed-set hash, embedding model) and only
recompute on change; investigate store-open time after B451's cleanup and
compaction shrinks the store (much of the 7.7 GB is orphan reifier garbage).
