# B478 - Conversation De-dup Merges Identical Text From Different Speakers

State: open (found 2026-10-10 while building B477)
Priority: P3

Plan: (inline — this card is self-contained)

### Problem Statement

`GraphGateway._bundle_conversation` (B454) de-duplicates repeated turns
keeping the newest, keyed on the turn **text alone**. Two different
speakers who say the identical sentence ("Same here!", "I love hiking")
collapse to whichever was said last, so the conversation section can
attribute a statement to the wrong person. Under B472 Phase 1b the speaker
is part of what was said and embedded, so it should be part of the key.

### Proposed Fix

Key the de-dup on `(speaker, normalized text)`; turns without a speaker
keep today's text-only key. Unit test: two speakers, identical text, both
survive; same speaker repeated, newest kept.

### Measure

Read path: T0 replay (`eval_gate.py`) on the DMR and LoCoMo-10 golden
stores. Expected effect is small; merge if within noise and tests pass
(it is a correctness fix).
