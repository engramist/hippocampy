# B548: the graph votes in fusion

**Status:** Built behind `[retrieval] graph_vote` (env `CAMPY_GRAPH_VOTE`, default off; member of the principled profile). Needs `question_analysis` (B547). Offline measurement below; no GPU gate on its own, gated in the whole principled-profile gate (B545).

**Principles:** P2, P8 (the answer is triangulated from vector, lexical and graph signals; a turn the graph says is the subject's fact about the asked-for kind now has a vote in which turns are shown).

**Why.** Reciprocal rank fusion ran per stage over vector and lexical lists only (`docs/pipeline-map.md`, stage 6). With B547's analysis, a third ranked list per question: evidence turns of Observations anchored on the subject, from one NamedQuery; fused with the other two in conversation seed selection.

## What it does

`thalamus.graph_vote_turns` (graph/queries/thalamus.py) takes the subject (`sids`), the asked kind (`kind_ids`, B547 `kind_id`), the named Concepts (`ent_ids`) and `need_kind`, and returns message URIs best first:

* anchored by `Observation -HAS_SUBJECT-> subject`, live (not archived, not `DEPRECATED_BY`), evidence turn by `EVIDENCED_BY`;
* kind match: `Observation -HAS_OBJECT-> e`, `e -INSTANCE_OF-> k`, `k -BROADER*-> asked kind` (B546's KX shape);
* entity match: `Observation -OBSERVATION_ABOUT-> a Concept the question names` (B554's evidence links write these);
* strict: when a kind is asked (`need_kind` = 1, bound into the query's `HAVING`) only kind matches are returned; otherwise a kind or entity match;
* rank: kind match, entity match, support (distinct facts the turn evidences), newest evidence date, message id (determinism).

The top 6 (`gateway.GRAPH_VOTE_TOP`) enter `GraphGateway._bundle_conversation_impl`'s fusion at weight 1 (`1/(60+rank)`), and a graph turn skips the B459 lexical-only gate. The list is built in `bundle_compiler._stage_conversation` (`thalamus/graph_vote.py`) and passed as the optional `graph_turns` parameter, only when non-empty.

No vote (the two-list path, byte-identical): flag off; `question_analysis` off (no analysis); no resolved subject; a tie (no dual anchors); neither a kind asked nor a Concept named; the query fails. Subject-only turns are never proposed. The full-history path (a small store that fits the budget) and the Observation stage are untouched.

## Offline measurement (copy of the R105 store with B544 admission, then the four backfills: roles, typed objects, WordNet kinds, evidence links; LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50, categories 1-4, 300 questions, 292 with gold turns)

Recall is the share of gold turns in the conversation stage's selection, averaged per question; seeds are the top 6, section adds the neighbour windows.

| | main | graph vote | gained / lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| seeds | 0.437 | 0.458 | +15 / -3 |
| section | 0.585 | 0.596 | +13 / -5 |
| conversation section tokens (mean) | 958 | 982 | |

Over all bundle sections, with the harness's own text-level recall: 0.588 to 0.603 (+13 / -4 over 292); total bundle tokens (mean) 1730 to 1754; 104 of 300 rendered prompts change.

The list is non-empty on 142 of 300 questions (13 ties, 1 without a subject); the selection changes on 104. Alone it holds 58 of 282 gold turns in its top 6 (precision at 6: 0.22). Weight 2 loses everywhere; a larger cutoff adds losses. Decision: strict, weight 1, top 6.

Entity restriction (coordinator's request: only Concepts carrying `ner_label` or `INSTANCE_OF`, decided by the graph's own typing): measured and not kept.

| | seeds | section | changed | gained / lost (section) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| any named Concept (kept) | 0.458 | 0.596 | 104 | +13 / -5 |
| tagger-labelled or kind-typed only | 0.452 | 0.592 | 67 | +7 / -3 |

The restriction removes generic noise and also real matches (an untyped "adoption" or "kids" Concept); net gain halves. It is a one-line `UNION` if a gate says otherwise.

Flag off: the rendered prompt is identical to main's for 300 of 300 questions.
