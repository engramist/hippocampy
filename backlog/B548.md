# B548: the graph votes in fusion

**Status:** Open. Design first.

**Principles:** P2, P8 (the answer is triangulated from vector, lexical and graph signals; today the graph has no vote in which turns are shown).

**Why.** Reciprocal rank fusion runs per stage over vector and lexical lists only (`docs/pipeline-map.md`, stage 6). A turn that the graph says is the subject's fact about the asked-for kind ranks no higher than any other turn. With B547's analysis, a third ranked list per question: evidence turns of Observations anchored on the subject (and, when a kind is asked, whose object is under that kind), from a NamedQuery; fused with the other two in conversation seed selection.

**Measure (offline):** evidence recall on the gate questions, gained and lost, and the tokens; then with the profile (B545).
