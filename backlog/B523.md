# B523: Ownership decided by a first-person possession rule, not by the extractor's verb

**Status:** Open — gate pending (R102 vs R101 on conv-42,conv-43,conv-47)

Lever 1b of the object-linking work, on top of B522 (HAS_SUBJECT / HAS_OBJECT). On a copy of a real
benchmark store the speaker's two dogs exist as Concepts `INSTANCE_OF` pet, but the extractor filed them
under `plans`, `prefers`, `relates_to` and `has_attribute`; `objects_of_subject(<speaker>, "owns")` returned
nothing, and the `owns` Observations said "dog" and "pup" with no entity. "How many pets does X have" cannot
be a query while the predicate depends on the verb a model happened to choose.

1. **Rule** (`campy/brain/temporal_lobe/observations/ownership.py`), behind `[observations] ownership_rule`
   (env `CAMPY_OBSERVATIONS_OWNERSHIP_RULE`, default off). It reads a user Message with the spaCy model the
   NER step already loads. Five named constructions, recorded per firing and documented in the module
   docstring: **possessive** ("my dog Toby", "Toby, my pup", "Toby is my dog"), **acquisition** ("I adopted
   Buddy", "I bought a bike named X"), **care** ("lucky to have Scout", "love having X"), **naming** ("I named
   him X", "X is what I named him", "his name is X", when the pronoun refers to a kind noun the speaker
   possessed or acquired in the same or the speaker's previous turn) and **coordination** ("X and I went",
   which only confirms: X must already be a rule-written owns object of the speaker, or share a possessable
   kind with one; the backfill runs it as a second pass). Verb set: have, own, adopt, get, buy, rescue.
2. **Entity.** An existing non-kind Concept linked to the Message by `MENTIONS` or by `OBSERVATION_ABOUT`
   of an Observation the Message evidences (`observations.message_concepts`), and a proper noun in the
   sentence. A proper noun with no linked Concept is reused by normalised key or created by the rule
   (the turn grounds it; `entities._concept_for`), linked to the Message, and, when the construction has
   a head noun ("my dog Pixie"), linked to that kind through `entities.resolve_kind`. No LLM.
3. **Skips, counted by reason:** question, negated verb, modal/wish/conditional/planned, possessor that is
   not first person ("your dog Toby", "my friend's dog Rex"), possessed noun that is a person role
   ("my friend Sam", "my gf, Toby"), "got Sam to help", wanting/planning ("I want to name him X"), no possessed kind before a naming, an unconfirmed "X and I", preference nouns ("my favorite X", "go-to"), no named speaker.
4. **Write.** Through `record_observation` (grounding, SHACL gate, dedupe on subject/predicate/object):
   predicate `owns`, subject = the speaker Concept, object = the entity, `HAS_SUBJECT` / `HAS_OBJECT`,
   `EVIDENCED_BY` the Message, quote = the matched span, `extraction_method` `pattern` (the existing
   value for non-LLM writers), `rule_version` `rule:first_person_possession`, confidence 0.95. No new
   predicate or edge: the reader is B522's `observations.objects_of_subject`.
5. **Where.** `process_turn` runs it after the extractor and its entity linking (also on turns the
   extractor skips, since it needs no LLM); `scripts/backfill_observation_roles.py --ownership [--sample N]`
   runs it over existing Messages (`ownership.backfill_ownership`), idempotent.

Serves Principles 3 (a rule decides ownership, the model only advised), 8 (a typed subject-to-object
edge), 4 (a grammar construction, nothing names a kind of thing) and 7 (flag, off by default). Gate
pending.

## Fixed: the `ownable` verdict with WordNet installed

With WordNet present the first-sense test let "Birch has always been my haven" fire ("haven" is
first a shelter, `noun.artifact`) and missed the group veto for "my group called ..." (group.n.01
is filed under `noun.Tops`). Fixed by construction:
- **"X is my N" needs a graph kind.** The copular form is where metaphors live ("my haven", "my
  rock"), so WordNet alone does not make N ownable there; the noun must be a kind some entity in
  the graph is INSTANCE_OF. "My dog Pixie", "I bought a guitar called Nova" keep the WordNet route.
- **A unique beginner takes the category it heads.** A `noun.Tops` first sense (group, animal,
  food, person) takes the lexname most of its hyponyms carry (group.n.01 -> `noun.group`).
- **Group nouns stay vetoed**, including "company": WordNet files company, business, team, family
  and club under groups, and "my company" is usually where the speaker works, so the rule does not
  write `owns` for it (P3: when the construction is ambiguous, the rule does not write).

Tests now fix WordNet's verdict per word (`lexicon` fixture: no WordNet / WordNet first senses), so
they agree with and without NLTK. Replay on store copies with WordNet installed: R102 3 firings ->
1 ("I named her Tilly"; "Nature's always been my haven" and "my group called 'Finding Home'"
dropped); R98 8 -> 8, unchanged.
