# B564: capture writes the roles of an event fact (action, with, place)

**Status:** Implemented behind `[observations] roles` (env `CAMPY_OBSERVATION_ROLES`, default off, in no profile). Not gated yet: the step-1 gate runs on a re-captured conv-26 (see `learnings/step1-capture-roles.md`, section 5). Plan step 1, 2026-11-10.

**Principles:** P1 (target: the step-1 gate and, in step 3, LoCoMo categories 1-4), P2 (kinds, partners and places are edges a query walks), P3 (the model proposes spans, a rule confirms, a WordNet synset is the id), P7 (one flag), P8 (every fact hangs off its subject by typed roles).

**What.** The extraction form gains `action` (did / plans only), `with` (a list) and `place`, each copied from the turn. `temporal_lobe/observations/roles.py` confirms each span (whole words in the turn; inside the quote of its fact, or in its clause when no other fact of the turn holds it) and writes, through registered NamedQueries only:

- `HAS_ACTION` Observation to an action Concept (`wn:<synset>`, `kind_scheme "action"`), chosen by `lexicon/wordnet.py: action_sense` (most frequent event noun sense; light verbs and words without a sense get none); its hypernym chain up to `event.n.01` is written as `BROADER` with `link_source "wordnet"`.
- `HAS_PARTICIPANT` to the speaker or an admitted PERSON / ORG / NORP Concept, else a nameless `ObjectPhrase` whose head is under person.n.01 / social_group.n.01.
- `HAS_PLACE` to an admitted GPE / LOC / FAC Concept, else an `ObjectPhrase` whose head is under location, structure, geological formation, body of water or vegetation.
- The B546 object path also takes `has_attribute` / `is_a` values that are common nouns under physical_entity.n.01.

**Reads** (`graph/queries/roles.py`): `observations.role_fill` (counts by standing and by "shared"), `facts.by_action_kind` (a `BROADER*` path from the fact's action to the kind asked about), `facts.with_participant`, `facts.participants_of`, `facts.at_place`, `facts.places_of`. Each row carries the quote, the evidence turn id and its date; an empty result is "not recorded".

**Decisions.**

- Action Concepts have no embedding and are in no vector or text index. They are excluded from the text lookups (`concept_links.live_concepts`, `orchestrator.find_*_concept`) and from `kinds.mark_kind`, so a word never resolves to one and a writer never turns one into a kind or an object. Step 3 resolves question verbs through the same WordNet sense function and `roles.action_concept`. `scripts/backfill_vector_index.py` skips them.
- The rule runs once per turn after every fact is written, because the clause rule needs every quote of the turn.
- No backfill: the spans need the model, so the flag applies to newly captured turns.

**Not done here.** The gate (conv-26 re-capture: anchored 60%+, shared 45%+, at most 20% wrong senses of 50 hand-checked, side-effect replay) and any default flip. Known sense limits to read in the hand check: WordNet's most frequent sense of "run" is the baseball one; phrasal verbs use the verb's head.

**Tests.** `tests/test_b564_roles.py`; flag-off byte identity is pinned by `tests/fixtures/b564_flagoff_golden.json` (captured before the change).
