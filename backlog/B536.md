# B536: a matching question turn seeds the reply that follows it

**Status:** Closed — replay showed 5 gained / 9 lost

**Lever:** reply pairing through the graph (`FOLLOWED_BY` walk from a matching "?" turn to the next turn by another speaker). Target: the retrieval misses where the evidence is a reply (about 108 of 282 missing evidence turns follow a question by the other speaker).

**Replay** (all 300 LoCoMo questions, R98 conv-30/44 and R100 conv-42/43/47, store copies, main code and gate flags; baseline 153/300 full evidence recall):

| Variant | Evidence turns gained | Lost | Questions to full recall | From full recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question's slot replaced by its reply (K = 6) | 14 | 13 | 5 | 9 |
| Replies added, no displacement | 12 | 9 | 5 | 5 |
| Replies added, K = 30 (ceiling) | 30 | 20 | 10 | 9 |

**Why:** the question turns that rank are on-topic and generic ("What is your favourite movie?"), so their replies are generic too, and they crowd out the specific evidence turn. Added replies also spend the shared cap of 18 on neighbour windows. The question already renders before the reply, as its d=1 neighbour.

**Follow-up idea (unmeasured):** pair only a top-1 question match, and give replies their own slots outside the neighbour cap.
