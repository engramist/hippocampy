# B527: model-extracted claims are shown to the answer model as facts

**Status:** Open

Principle 3: anything a model writes into memory is a candidate until a rule or an independent source confirms it. Today the bundle's `semantic` section shows LLM-extracted Observations as "claims the user made", whether or not a rule confirmed them.

**Found by opening a real answer prompt** (2026-10-25, stack B522–B524, store copy of conv-30/conv-44). For "How many pets will Andrew have?" the section showed `[Andrew] owns: dog ("That photo of you with your dog is so cute")`. That is the other speaker's dog. Other extractor rows on the same store have the wrong subject too: "Toby owns Buddy", "my dogs owns me".

**Work:**
1. Measure first. On a store copy, count extractor Observations per predicate whose quote contradicts the subject: a second-person or third-person possessive on the object, or a subject that is not the speaker for a first-person quote.
2. A confirmation rule, written as a named construction, marks each Observation `confirmed` or `candidate`. The semantic section then either shows only confirmed claims, or labels candidates as such. Choose by gate result.
3. Gate on the conv-42/43/47 holdout, `Targets in gate: all`.
