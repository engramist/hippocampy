# B551: the harness's instruction sentence is part of the retrieval query

**Status:** Open. Found by the B547 design screen.

**Principles:** P6 (measure correctly), P5.

**What was seen.** The benchmark harness appends an instruction sentence to category-2 (temporal) questions. Retrieval embeds, full-text searches and resolves Concepts over the whole string: 84 of 676 Concepts resolved over the 300 gate questions are "conversation" (12%), all from that sentence. B547's analysis cuts the question at its first `?`.

**To do.** Check how the harness passes the instruction (campy-benchmarks locomo10 runner) and whether published LoCoMo setups send it as part of the question. The retrieval query should be the question; the instruction belongs with the answer prompt. Fix where it belongs (harness or `ask` split of query and instruction), behind a flag in the engine if there, and record which it is in results provenance. Measure: evidence recall and category-2 accuracy on the gate slice.
