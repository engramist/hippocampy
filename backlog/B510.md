# B510: precise graph walk — strong anchors, only-missing facts, small cap within budget (flag, default off)

**Status:** open (PR pending gate) · Serves principles 3 (rules decide), 4 (generalise), 5 (structure, not wording) and 6 (measure before believing). Replaces B503's retrieval walk when `CAMPY_GRAPH_WALK_V2=1`; the link writers (B503/B504/B508/B509) are untouched.

## Problem

B503's walk (`graph_linking_limit` 12) was gated on fresh-ingest LoCoMo-10 conv-30 + conv-44 (60 questions each, categories 1-4, fields mode, same answer model and judge in every run):

| Run | Setup | Score |
|---|---|---|
| R88 | main | 0.525 |
| R95 | full foundation stack, walk on | 0.467 (7 gained, 14 lost vs R88) |
| R96 | same stack, `CAMPY_GRAPH_LINKING_LIMIT=0` (no walk section) | 0.517 (7 gained, 8 lost vs R88) |

R95 and R96 differ only by the walk. The B502 gather pass failed the same way (17 gained, 40 lost on the LoCoMo replay): more loosely-related context hurts this answer model.

## Part A: why the walk lost (replay of R95, retrieval only)

Method: the kept R95 store was copied (never opened in place). Conv-44 questions were replayed on the full store, which is what they ran against; conv-30 questions on a copy with conv-44's messages, observations and concepts removed, which is what they ran against. The replay reproduces R95's recorded context sections exactly for 119 of 120 questions (section item counts; mean token delta 1.3), so the section contents below are what the answer model saw. The result file keeps only item counts and the top 8 items, so the linked items themselves come from the replay.

**What the section was.** 111 of 120 questions got a `linked` section: 12 items (10.8 on average), about 530 tokens, a 44% larger bundle (1,111 to 1,601 tokens). Of the 1,203 items: 56% were reached only through a speaker's name or a concept linked to 10% or more of the messages (75% for the losses below, 94% for the gains); 81% came from the last two sessions (the walk keeps the newest items of a hop, so what it adds is recency, not relevance); 1% had a source message that was already in the conversation section; and for only 6 of 120 questions did the section add an evidence turn the conversation lacked. The conversation section is byte-identical with and without the walk (evidence recall unchanged): nothing is displaced inside the bundle. The section is inserted between the semantic and conversation sections, so what it displaces is the answer model's attention, not turns.

**Which questions the walk changed** (R95 vs R96, the like-for-like pair; 10 questions):

| Question | R96 to R95 | Anchors that produced the items | On topic? | Evidence in linked | What the section did |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| conv-30 q41 favourite dancing memory | pass to fail | "dancing" (18 msgs, rare; Gina dropped by the 20% rule) | topical, not specific | no (already in conversation) | 12 chat turns about dancing; answer correct but padded, judged wrong |
| conv-44 q6 when Audrey adopted Pixie | pass to fail | "Audrey" (13.5%, a speaker, under the 20% rule); "Pixie" (2 msgs) | no | no | all 12 items came through "Audrey", newest first; Pixie's own turns were already shown, so the real anchor added nothing; answer abstained |
| conv-44 q15 shared dog frustration | pass to fail | "my dogs" (184 msgs, 17.6%) | no | no | the newest dog chat; answer changed |
| conv-44 q11 rock climbing date | pass to fail | "Andrew" (11.3%, a speaker); "rock climbing" (covered) | no | no | the newest Andrew turns; wrong date |
| conv-44 q30 beach trip date | pass to fail | "Andrew" (speaker); "my girlfriend" | no | no | newest Andrew turns; abstained |
| conv-44 q74 feeling about the housing search | pass to fail | "Andrew" (speaker); "place" | no | no | newest Andrew turns; wrong sentiment |
| conv-44 q38 wine tasting date | pass to fail | "Andrew" (speaker); "wine tasting" (covered) | no | no | newest Andrew turns; abstained |
| conv-44 q35 names of Andrew's dogs | fail to pass | "my dogs" (a kind with 5 instances) | yes | yes (1 of 3 evidence turns) | the one real multi-hop gain |
| conv-44 q31 what Andrew did with his dogs | fail to pass | "my dogs" | partly | no | dog chat; plausible, not shown to be causal |
| conv-44 q64 flower of Audrey's tattoo | fail to pass | "Audrey" (speaker) | no | no | irrelevant turns; the answer came from the semantic section: noise |

The other 7 of R95's 14 losses against R88 also fail in R96, and 4 of its 7 gains (the conv-30 temporal ones) also pass in R96, so they are not the walk's doing. Net, the walk was 3 gains against 7 losses; the 3 gains come from one real hit.

**Findings.** (1) The anchors were speaker names and common nouns: participants (Audrey 13.5%, Andrew 11.3%) slipped under the 20% rule, and "my dogs" (17.6%) is a kind that fills the conversation. (2) A strong anchor ("Pixie", "rock climbing", "wine tasting") already had its turns in the conversation section, so it contributed nothing of its own while a weak anchor filled the 12 slots. (3) Selection was newest-first, which is unrelated to the question. (4) The section grew the prompt by half instead of replacing weaker context. All four are structural, none is a wording issue.

## Change (flag `[retrieval] graph_walk_v2` / `CAMPY_GRAPH_WALK_V2=1`, default off)

With the flag on the v2 walk runs instead of the B503 walk (`thalamus.bundle_concept_walk_v2`, `graph/walk_v2.py`, `_stage_concept_walk_v2` in the bundle compiler). The v1 query, its `graph_linking_limit` and the flag-off bundle are untouched; v2 also needs the links, so it is meant to run with `graph_linking` on at write time.

1. **Strong anchors only.** Of the Concepts the question names, only these anchor the walk:
   - a typed **entity**: it has an outgoing `INSTANCE_OF` (or legacy `IS_A`) to a kind, which is what entity extraction writes;
   - a **kind with 2 to 12 instances**: the kind anchors nothing itself, its instances do ("my dogs" reaches Toby, Buddy, Scout).

   Never an anchor: an untyped Concept ("dates", "my plan", "this class"); a **participant** (the Concept's text is a Message `speaker`; the rule is structural, a person speaking in the conversation is its background, not a topic); an entity linked to more than `graph_walk_v2_max_share` (default **10%**) of the messages; a kind whose reach (distinct messages mentioning the kind or any instance) exceeds that share (it saturates the store, so cannot discriminate); a kind with one instance or more than twelve. Below B470's floor of 50 messages a share means nothing and there is no cap.

   Why 10% and not the existing 20%: on R95 the concepts in the 10-20% band were Audrey (13.5%), Andrew (11.3%), "my dogs" (17.6%) and "studio" (12.7%), and three of the four were the junk above. Measured with the cap at 20% instead of 10%, the number of the 120 questions with something to add is 32 instead of 12 (kind anchors go from 20 to 44), the extra ones being kinds such as the dog kind that saturate the conversation.
2. **Only what is missing.** An anchor (for a kind, each instance) that the conversation section already mentions is dropped: a shown turn is linked to it, or its words occur in a shown turn. When every anchor is covered the walk returns nothing. A candidate turn whose **source message** is already shown (a conversation turn, or the `evidence_ref` of a semantic Observation) is dropped, so a turn and the Observation extracted from it are one item. Not evidence: archived turns, assistant turns, questions, echoes of the question.
3. **Small cap, ordered by link strength.** At most `graph_walk_v2_limit` (default 4). Order: hop 1 (the anchor's own mentions; an instance's mentions for a kind) before hop 2 (siblings of an entity under a small kind; instances of narrower kinds of a kind anchor); then more anchors hit; then cosine to the question; then id. Not recency.
4. **Participant scope.** When the question names a participant, only turns from sessions that person spoke in qualify (another conversation's turn does not enter through a shared kind such as "activity" or "city").
5. **Skip when covered, skip when the conversation holds the whole history** (as v1).
6. **Within the budget.** The prompt does not grow: conversation tokens plus linked tokens never exceed the conversation section alone (with no conversation section, `conversation_token_budget`). Items are admitted strongest first; for each, conversation turns are displaced weakest first. A turn's rank is its match rank (`keep_rank`; a neighbour turn ranks as its matched turn's rank plus 1.5 per step away). The `graph_walk_v2_protect` (3) best-matched turns are never displaced. An item that cannot be paid for is not added, and a closer turn is never swapped for a farther one (an item must be at least as close to the question, by cosine, as every turn it displaces).

Knobs: `graph_walk_v2_limit` (4), `graph_walk_v2_max_share` (0.10), `graph_walk_v2_protect` (3), `graph_walk_v2_min_similarity` (0.0, no floor). Env: `CAMPY_GRAPH_WALK_V2`, `CAMPY_GRAPH_WALK_V2_LIMIT`. The conversation queries take an optional `annotate_rank` parameter (set only with the flag on) that adds `keep_rank` to their rows; absent, their rows are unchanged.

No topic, question-pattern or wording rule anywhere: every test is on the graph (edges, degrees, speakers, sessions) or on token and cosine arithmetic.

## Offline estimate (retrieval only, no model; the 120 R95 questions on the R95 store copies)

Setup: the foundation stack's flags plus `CAMPY_GRAPH_WALK_V2=1`; the baseline is the same stack with the walk off (R96's bundle).

| | v1 walk (R95) | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| questions with a linked section | 111 of 120 | **10 of 120 (8%)** |
| items per question that has one | 10.8 | **2.0** |
| tokens added per question that has one | about 530 | **80** (7 averaged over all 120) |
| average bundle tokens (baseline 1,111) | 1,601 | **1,109** (never above the baseline) |
| questions whose bundle differs from the baseline | 111 | **10** (110 are identical) |
| conversation turns displaced | 0 | 19 turns in 10 questions, all neighbour turns or match rank 3 or lower |
| **evidence turns displaced** | 0 | **0** |
| evidence turns the section adds that the conversation lacked | 6 questions | 0 |

Funnel for the 120 questions: 119 resolve at least one Concept; 67 have a strong anchor (87 anchors, 20 of them kinds); in 48 of those the conversation already mentions every anchor (skip); 19 have an uncovered anchor; 12 have a missing candidate; 10 survive the budget and closeness check. Concepts rejected, by reason, over all questions: participant 131, untyped 127, saturating kind 24, too common 8.

Reading it: the walk is now close to a no-op on LoCoMo, by construction. It reproduces R96's bundles on 110 of 120 questions and adds a couple of turns on the rest. It gives up the one real v1 gain (conv-44 q35, "my dogs" is a saturating kind in a conversation about dogs); that case is what the conversation stage's neighbours are meant to cover. The flag is expected to be about neutral on LoCoMo; its intended lever is multi-session aggregation (LongMemEval multi-session) where the question names a specific entity or a small kind whose turns are spread over sessions and the conversation stage does not show them. The offline replay cannot show that.
