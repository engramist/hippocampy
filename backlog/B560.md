# B560: the concept walk is anchored on the question's subject, through a `SPOKEN_BY` edge

**Status:** Implemented. The walk runs only under the `principled` profile (`graph_linking_limit` 12; main's default is 0), so the anchored query replaces the old walk there and needs no walk flag. The new capture edge is behind `[loop] spoken_by` (env `CAMPY_SPOKEN_BY`, default off, in the principled profile). Gated in the whole principled-profile gate (B545). Found in the R106 loss breakdown (2026-11-07).

**Principles:** P1 (target: the 161 LoCoMo questions whose linked section was at its cap), P2 (the anchor, the order and the cap are one SPARQL query; no Python filtering), P8 (a turn hangs off its speaker by a typed edge; the edge capture did not write is written, not approximated by a string match).

**What was seen.** R106 ran the principled profile against main on LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50: judge accuracy 0.503 to 0.427 (8 gained, 31 lost). 27 of the 31 losses are among the 161 questions where the walk's linked section is at its 12-item cap (accuracy 0.466 to 0.335, abstentions 4% to 19%). 63% of the linked turns came from other conversations (the walk query ran over the whole store), and 177 of 230 linked sections held a turn dated after every conversation turn, under a header that says later dates supersede earlier ones. The walk carried gold in 2 to 11 of roughly 2,400 to 3,600 items.

**Rule.** The walk returns a user turn only if the question's subject spoke it (`Message -SPOKEN_BY-> subject Concept`) and it links (`MENTIONS`) to a walk seed (B559's seeds: the Concepts the question names other than its subject) at hop 1, or at hop 2 (one SKOS step: `IS_A` / `INSTANCE_OF` / `BROADER` parent, child or sibling, the `BROADER` closure and its instances). Order: hop, then distinct seeds linked, then the subject's Observations the turn evidences (`HAS_SUBJECT` and `EVIDENCED_BY`), then newest, then URI. Cap `graph_linking_limit` (12; at most 50). Shown oldest first. Messages only: Observations are the Observation stage's. The turns already in the bundle, archived turns and assistant turns are left out inside the query.

The walk adds nothing when there is no question analysis, no resolved subject, a tie, an unresolved name outranking the subject, or no seed. A first-person question that names nobody adds nothing (follow-up below).

**The edge.** `Message -SPOKEN_BY-> Concept`, plain, written by the Loop in the step that already seeds a named speaker's Person Concept (B472 Phase 2), so no second Concept is created; a role or placeholder speaker ("user", "assistant", "Speaker 1") gets none. `scripts/backfill_spoken_by.py <store>` links an existing store (dry run by default; `--apply` writes; idempotent; looks the Concept up and never creates one; lists a named speaker with no Concept).

**Removed.** `GraphGateway._bundle_concept_walk` (the store-wide Python walk) and its use of the B470 common-term test (the test itself stays: the lexical query reads it), and `_neg_str`. The un-anchored walk with `question_analysis` off is gone: without an analysis there is no subject, so the walk adds nothing.

**Header.** Unchanged ("later dates supersede earlier ones"): linked turns are now the subject's own, and no linked turn is dated after the conversation's last turn (45.9% before). 102 of 179 linked sections still hold a turn dated after every turn of the conversation section; those are the subject's later statements in the same conversation.

## Measurement

Offline replay of `compile_bundle` plus `_bundle_to_prompt` on a copy of the R106 store (LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50, 300 questions, 292 with gold turns), after `backfill_spoken_by` (2,840 of 2,840 turns linked; second run writes nothing). Against the main walk: +29 gained, -3 lost. No gap to the label-join prototype (+0.0018; the 4 fewer linked sections are questions whose only linked items were Observations, now dropped). On the 161 questions at the cap in R106: pooled recall 0.7040 against 0.6021 with the walk off (+30 / -0); 148 have a linked section (113 at the cap, mean 10.7 items); 13 have none, so their prompt equals the walk-off prompt. Variants measured on the prototype (cap 6: 0.6506; cap 20: 0.6877 at 854 tokens; recency order: 0.6524) kept the cap at 12 and the order above.

Profile main: the rendered prompts of this branch and of origin/main are byte-identical on the same store copy, 300 of 300.

Recall is not judge accuracy. In R106 even questions whose linked turns were 0 to 25% foreign scored 0.444 against main's 0.556 (n=45), so anchoring may not recover the loss by itself; the gate is principled with the anchored walk against walk off.

## Open

* Personal store: no speaker labels, so no `SPOKEN_BY` edge and no subject Concept; the anchor there is the user's own unlabelled turns for a first-person question that names nobody. Needs a first-person signal in `QuestionAnalysis`. Not built; the walk adds nothing for such a question.
* Conversation scope: two conversations that share a speaker name share one Person Concept, so the anchor admits both people's turns. The graph has no conversation node to separate them. The R106 store has no such overlap.
* `SPOKEN_BY` is written by the Loop only; a path that stores turns without running the Loop is covered by the backfill.

**Tests.** `tests/test_b560_anchored_walk.py`: the flag; the Loop writes the edge under the flag and not without it, none for a generic speaker; the backfill (dry run, idempotent, never creates a Concept); the walk keeps only the subject's turns (with the scope-bug regression); nothing without subject or seed; hop 2; the order; shown, archived and assistant turns out; the stage adds nothing without an anchor and never runs the query; the cap; profile main runs no walk.
