# B578: a cross-encoder reranks the conversation section's turns (flag)

**Status:** Built 2026-11-18, behind `[retrieval] turn_rerank` (off, in no profile). Its GPU gate is approved by the review session to run after this merges.

**Principles:** P1 (gated on the 809), P2 (vectors.db stays an index; nothing stored), P3 (the reader sees verbatim, source-linked turns; graph-vote turns are not overridden by a model score), P4 (ND-5's construction, no knob tuned on the 809), P5 (a retrieval change, not wording), P7 (behind a flag).

**Why.** ND-3 and ND-4 showed the missing gold turns sit in a wider vector pool but rank low on the question's vector. ND-5 (offline) reranked the control's turns plus the top 100 by vector with a small cross-encoder and cut to the section's own size. Result: 46.0% of the no-gold questions gain a gold turn, 6.9% of the all-gold questions lose one, net estimate +20.5 questions by bucket moves.

**What.**
- `campy/brain/thalamus/turn_rerank.py`: the stage. Candidates are the section's turns plus the top 100 live user Messages by vector (`thalamus.bundle_conversation_rerank_candidates`). Each is scored as "speaker: text", the section's own turn count is kept, and graph-vote turns (B548, the top GRAPH_VOTE_TOP) are pinned. Presented in the section's order.
- Called from `_stage_conversation`; a full-history section is left as it is.
- Config: `turn_rerank` (env `CAMPY_TURN_RERANK`), `turn_rerank_model`, and `turn_rerank_path` (env `CAMPY_TURN_RERANK_PATH`, a local folder). There is no download; a missing model leaves the section unchanged, with one warning.
- Reported in the profile flags (PROFILE_STAYS_ON).

**Gate.** Step 4's principled stores, both arms at one commit, flag off against on. Pass above +1.9 points on the 809; kill at 0 or below, or category-5 correct refusals down more than 5 points.

**Done when:** the gate is judged and the flag is either on by default or deleted.
