# B538: relative dates in user turns resolved by rule at capture and shown inline

**Status:** Built behind `[observations] turn_dates` (env `CAMPY_TURN_DATES`, default off). Offline answer replay nets +3 (6 gained / 3 lost), below the +5 bar set before the gate, so it is not flipped on and no gate has run.

**Principles:** P3 (rules compute dates, the model never does the arithmetic), P5 (presentation structure, not prompt wording), P7 (flag, one lever).

**Lever and target:** the 20 category-2 failures that are date arithmetic (9) or a temporal abstention with the evidence present (11), plus no regression on the 44 category-2 questions that pass. Cause (breakdown 5.2): of 22 failed date questions with a parseable gold, a resolved time equal to the gold was in the store for 7 and in the prompt for 2; 11 had observations with no `time_text`; the raw turn showed "yesterday" under a session-date stamp.

## What it does

1. **Capture.** For every stored user turn, `temporal_lobe/observations/turn_dates.py` finds relative time expressions (`yesterday`, `tomorrow`, `the day before yesterday`, `last night`, `N units ago`, `in N units`, `last|next|this <weekday>`, `last|next|this week|month|year|weekend`) and resolves each with `timeparse.normalize_time` against the turn's `occurred_at` (else `created_at`). Not annotated: expressions that resolve to the turn's own day (`today`, `tonight`, `now`), bare weekdays, vague amounts, rolling windows (`the last year`, `the next month`). Each expression is a `TimeMention` node hung off its Message by the plain edge `HAS_TIME_MENTION`, with `time_text`, `span_start`, `span_end` and the typed literals `time_start`, `time_end`, `time_precision` (the same as an Observation, B484). Written in `notify_turn` and by `backfill_turn_mentions(db)` (idempotent: the mention id is the turn plus its span, and the upsert rewrites in place).
2. **Rendering.** The conversation and `linked` sections, and the quoted evidence inside an Observation, show the resolved date right after the phrase: `we went yesterday [= 2023-05-07]`. The Observation `when:` uses the same form: `(when: last week [= week of 2022-04-04])` (was `last week → week of 2022-04-04`).
3. **Prompt.** One clause appended to those section descriptions, only with the flag on: a bracketed `[= date]` is the phrase resolved by rule against the date of its turn.

## Graph design

Properties written: `TimeMention.{mention_id,time_text,span_start,span_end,time_start,time_end,time_precision,archived,created_at,source,authority}` and the edge `HAS_TIME_MENTION` (Message to TimeMention). Read by the registered query `turn_mentions.for_messages` (SPARQL; the bundle renderer calls it for the turns it is about to show); written by `turn_mentions.upsert`; backfill candidates by `turn_mentions.list_user_turns`. No model writes a query or a mention.

## Measured before any build decision

**(a) Resolver against gold** (cat-2 questions of R98 and R100, evidence turns of each question, gold parsed by the same rules, typos in gold spacing repaired). Best annotation on any evidence turn:

| | exact | overlapping | wrong | none |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| timeparse spans, all 64 parseable golds | 35 | 5 | 3 | 21 |
| dateparser on the same spans | 13 | 22 | 2 | 27 |
| dateparser `search_dates` on the turn | 13 | 26 | 17 | 8 |
| timeparse, the 17 parseable targeted failures | 9 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| dateparser on the same spans, same 17 | 5 | 5 | 0 | 7 |

dateparser returns a point, so its "overlapping" is a point inside the gold week or month; it cannot produce a range, and it has no result for 53 of 207 spans (for example `last Friday`). It never disagreed with timeparse where both resolved (60 same day, 94 inside timeparse's range). timeparse is kept; see B531.

Whole stores (5 conversations, 3,042 turns): 203 turns carry a resolved expression, 207 expressions.

**(b) Answer replay** (store copies, gate flags, the configured local answer model through `run_ask` with capture off, scored by the benchmark's own judge path, 3 votes; 64 questions: the 20 targeted failures and the 44 category-2 passes):

| | n | to pass | to fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| targeted failures | 20 | 6 | |
| passing questions | 44 | | 6 raw, 3 after replaying the flag-off prompt |

Of the 6 passes that failed, 3 also failed when replayed with the flag off (the recorded pass was answer noise), so against the same pipeline the net is 6 gained, 3 lost, +3. The 3 real losses are not clearly caused by the annotation (one cites a different date, one hedges "on or before", one abstains).

Reach: the gold date appears as an annotation in the prompt for 9 of the 20 targeted failures (2 before). 6 of those 9 are now right. The other 3 answer with the right date after opening "I don't have that information", which the harness scores as a non-answer. 6 of the 20 have no relative expression in the evidence turn ("two weekends later", "left after 3 years", a date that is the session date itself), so no rule on a relative phrase can reach them.

Gains: conv-30_q8, conv-30_q26, conv-42_q3, conv-47_q14, conv-47_q21, conv-47_q23. Losses against the flag-off replay: conv-30_q34, conv-42_q15, conv-43_q37.
