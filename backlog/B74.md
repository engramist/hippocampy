### B74 · Wire Valence Into Sweep Decay Logic

**Problem:** B69 implemented valence propagation for live retrieval (OUTCOME_SIGNAL edges boost/penalize `current_truth` results). However, the background sweep never reads `Plan.valence` or OUTCOME_SIGNAL edges. Negative plan outcomes don't affect concept `pathway_strength` during background processing. The system records outcomes but doesn't learn from them during background decay — only during live retrieval.

**What it does:** Adds a sweep phase that reads OUTCOME_SIGNAL edges and adjusts `pathway_strength` of target Concepts based on accumulated valence signals.

**Depends on:** B69

**Files to modify:**
- `mcp_engine/sweep.py` — add valence-aware decay/boost phase

**Adapter allow-list propagation check:** No tool changes — N/A.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Sweep reads OUTCOME_SIGNAL edges during each cycle
- Concepts with net negative valence have faster pathway_strength decay
- Concepts with net positive valence have slower pathway_strength decay (or slight boost)
- Formula documented in code: e.g., `decay_factor *= (1 + avg_valence * 0.3)` where avg_valence ∈ [-1, 1]
- `pytest tests/ -k sweep -q` passes
- No mutation of OUTCOME_SIGNAL edge properties (read-only from sweep)

**Outcome:** Background decay incorporates outcome learning — failed-plan concepts fade faster, successful-plan concepts persist longer.
