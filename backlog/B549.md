# B549: `ner_label` is evidence; an entity's standing is derived by rule

**Status:** Built behind `[loop] entity_standing` (env `CAMPY_ENTITY_STANDING`, default off; member of the principled profile; a no-op without `entity_admission`). Offline measurement below; no GPU gate on its own, gated in the whole principled-profile gate (B545). Found by the B547 design screen (2026-11-05) on a store with B544 admission; widened from PERSON to every admitted label by B554's R105 finding (Love, Nature, Music, Karlie, Mustang).

**Principles:** P3 (a tagger label is evidence, not a fact; a rule over independent signals writes the standing), P4 (no word list: every signal is structural or corpus-derived and was checked on conversations the rule was not built on), P8 (a fact hangs off a real entity, so "is this a person" is answered from the graph).

**What was seen.** spaCy labels chat words as named entities wrongly, on the R105 store (B544 on, 156 admitted entities): PERSON for Yoga, Art, Congrats, Glad, Awww, "Alright Sam", "Yep Deb"; WORK_OF_ART for Love, Nature, Sleep; ORG for Music, Switch, Tues, "Oof, Sam", Natarajasana, "Warrior II"; GPE for Karlie, Mustang. B544 stores the label as `ner_label`; its kind guard kept those Concepts out of the kind scheme (so "art" could never be a kind), and B547 could take the label as a person.

## What it does

`Concept.entity_standing` (`confirmed | doubtful | unverified`; absent = not assessed) is derived by rule (`hippocampus/entity_standing.py`; spaCy sentence boundaries and POS only, no word list, no WordNet, no model). The raw `ner_label` is untouched. Evidence pool: the user turns by the speakers of the sessions of the turns that mention the entity (a store without speakers pools its speakerless turns).

For each occurrence of the entity's tokens (a leading the/a/an dropped): *initial* when every token before it in its sentence is an interjection or punctuation, else *mid*. `mid` = written capitalised mid-sentence, `init` = capitalised sentence-initially, `low` = written lowercase.

```
a speaker of the conversation        -> confirmed
mid > 0 and mid >= low (a tie too)   -> confirmed   (a capital that position does not explain)
low > 0                              -> doubtful    (an ordinary word the tagger caught)
otherwise                            -> unverified  (capital only at sentence start, or no evidence)
```

The standing is relative to the label: it withdraws trust from non-names, it does not decide between person, place and organisation (Karlie GPE, Jasper PERSON stay confirmed).

**Span trimming, at admission.** A span of two or more tokens in a sentence-initial position whose leading tokens are alphabetic words or commas and whose remainder is a known name (a speaker of the conversation, or a Concept whose standing is confirmed) is replaced by the remainder: "Yep Deb" -> "Deb", "Oof, Sam" -> "Sam". POS cannot decide it ("Yep" is tagged a proper noun). The Loop applies it to step 1's entities before every later step, the Observation extractor through the same `admit_parsed`.

**When.** At admission, per turn: the standing of every entity the turn admitted or mentions is re-assessed over the evidence the graph holds then (evidence accrues; the standing follows it). Plus the idempotent backfill `scripts/backfill_entity_standing.py` (dry run by default): assess every labelled Concept, trim-merge wrappers (MENTIONS, OBSERVATION_ABOUT, HAS_SUBJECT, HAS_OBJECT move to the target; the wrapper is archived; its text becomes an alt label of the target), assess again. No sweep.

## Graph design

| Written | Where |
|---|---|
| `Concept.entity_standing` (string, new column, one property) | `entity_standing.set_standing` (replaces: derived, the last assessment wins); schema.py Concept DDL + migration list |
| trim-merge: `MENTIONS` / `OBSERVATION_ABOUT` / `HAS_SUBJECT` / `HAS_OBJECT` re-pointed, `Concept.archived`, `HAS_ALT_LABEL` | `entity_standing.repoint_*`, `quests.archive_concept`, `quests.create_alt_label` + `link_concept_has_alt_label` |

Read by (every reader decides inside its own named query, not by filtering in Python):

* `entity_admission.concept_status` returns `can_be_kind`: `!((ner_label bound && standing != "doubtful") || schema_org_type Person)`. `entity_admission.can_be_kind` returns it. A doubtful common word ("art", "music") may be a kind.
* `typed_objects.named_entity_texts` (the kind-label guard B546 repeats): `{ ?c campy:ner_label ?_l FILTER NOT EXISTS { ?c campy:entity_standing "doubtful" } } UNION { Person }`.
* `question_analysis.concept_lexicon` returns `person_basis`: `strong` (typed Person), `weak` (`ner_label` PERSON and the standing is `confirmed` or not recorded), else none. `load_lexicon` reads the basis. B547's question-side tagger agreement is unchanged.
* B554 linking and B548's vote do **not** read the standing (v1): 149 of 2,243 links would be dropped, about 20 of 25 sampled dropped links are correct topical links ("yoga classes").
* Evidence reads: `entity_standing.session_speakers`, `concept_speakers`, `turns_by_speakers`, `turns_unspoken`, `turn_entities`, `concept_turns`, `confirmed_texts`; the backfill audit `kind_conflicts` (a kind that is a confirmed entity is listed, never edited).

Flag off: no standing is written or trimmed; `concept_status`, `named_entity_texts` and `concept_lexicon` return what they returned (no standing recorded reads as before); 300 of 300 rendered prompts byte-identical to main.

## Measured (offline, no GPU, no LLM)

Design set: all 156 admitted entities of the R105 store, hand-marked by one rater (G real and label right 90, W real but label wrong 27, N not an entity 39); production backfill on a copy (112 confirmed, 25 doubtful, 14 unverified, 5 trimmed). "Flagged" = not confirmed (what B547 reads).

| | flagged vs N (tp / fp / fn) | precision | recall | N+W precision / recall | real flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all 156 | 38 / 6 / 1 | 0.86 | 0.97 | 0.98 / 0.65 | 1 of 90 (Sleep) |
| PERSON 72 | 24 / 1 / 0 | 0.96 | 1.00 | 1.00 / 0.89 | 0 of 44 |
| GPE 33 | 3 / 0 / 0 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 / 0.38 | 0 of 25 |
| ORG 26 | 6 / 3 / 1 | 0.67 | 0.86 | 1.00 / 0.45 | 0 of 6 |
| WORK_OF_ART 9 | 3 / 1 / 0 | 0.75 | 1.00 | 0.75 / 0.75 | 1 of 5 |

All 10 speakers confirmed (4 of them typed `Thing`). Doubtful or trimmed (the kind guard's reading): precision 0.90, recall 0.69 against N. Held-out (spaCy over the turns of conversations the rule was not built on, stratified hand samples): conv-30/42, 40 marked, precision 0.83 (N+W 0.94), weighted recall 0.94, 1 of 18 real names flagged; conv-43/44, 30 marked, precision 0.67 (N+W 0.87), weighted recall 0.71, 2 of 10 real names flagged. Held-out is weaker than the design set. Trimming transferred 3 of 3.

B547, 300 gate questions (conv-26/41/48/49/50, categories 1-4, 60 each, the runner's own loader, real `load_lexicon` on the store copy): subject or anchor changed on 0, concept list on 0; 25 of 66 weak persons stop being persons, 41 stay. Kind guard: 4 labelled topic Concepts become kind-eligible (Art, Music, Love, Nature); Yoga and pottery are already kinds carrying a PERSON label and become consistent.
