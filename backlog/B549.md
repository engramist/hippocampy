# B549: `ner_label` PERSON on things that are not people

**Status:** Open. Found by the B547 design screen (2026-11-05) on a store with B544 admission.

**Principles:** P3 (a tagger label is evidence, not a fact), P8.

**What was seen.** spaCy labels many chat words PERSON: Yoga, Art, pottery, Jasper, Aerosmith, Ratatouille, Kickboxing, Congrats, Awww, Glad, "Alright Sam", "Yep Deb" (about 60 on the R104 store). B544 stores the label as `ner_label`; its kind guard then keeps those Concepts out of the kind scheme (so "art" could never be a kind), and a question-side reader taking `ner_label` PERSON as a person anchored "What new yoga poses did Deborah try?" on Yoga.

**To do.** A person needs more than the tagger: a speaker of the conversation, or PERSON from at least two turns in sentences where it is a subject or object of a person verb (said, told, met, married), or a capitalised name the WordNet first sense does not call a non-person. Keep the raw tagger label as evidence; derive `is_person` by rule. The kind guard reads the rule, not the raw label. Measure on the R104 store copy: the ~60 above, and all speakers still persons.
