# B532: concept-label matches stay inside one sentence and match part of speech

**Status:** Open

Two cheap matcher ideas from a commercial vocabulary classifier:
- **Sentence scope.** A phrase or near match never crosses a sentence boundary.
- **Part of speech.** A lemma match only counts where the label and the text share a part of speech.

Our MENTIONS linking (`concept_links.match_concepts`) does not check either.

**Work:**
1. Count first. On a store copy, find MENTIONS edges whose matched span crosses a spaCy sentence boundary, and one-word labels matched with a different part of speech (e.g. noun label "bark" on verb "bark").
2. If either count is non-trivial, add the check behind a flag and gate on the holdout.
