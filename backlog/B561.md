# B561: the anchored walk adds nothing to first-person questions on a personal store

**Status:** Open. Follow-up from B560 (2026-11-07).

**Principles:** P8, P4 (the walk must work on the personal store, not only on LoCoMo).

**What.** B560 anchors the concept walk on `Message -SPOKEN_BY-> subject`. On a personal store, turns have no named speaker and most questions are first-person ("what did I decide about X?"), so B547 resolves no subject and the walk adds nothing. The user is the subject of such questions.

**To do (design first).** Give the user a subject Concept at capture, or anchor a turn with no speaker on "the user". Add `QuestionAnalysis.first_person` from the existing spaCy parse (pronoun tokens, syntax only), distinct from `subject_unresolved`. Measure on a synthetic personal store, because every LoCoMo turn has a speaker.
