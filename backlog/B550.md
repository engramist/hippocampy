# B550: nicknames resolve to their person

**Status:** Open. Found by the B547 design screen.

**Principles:** P8 (one subject, one entity), P3.

**What was seen.** "Mel", "Deb", "Cal", "Caro" are separate Concepts next to Melanie, Deborah, Calvin, Caroline; "How long have Mel and her husband been married?" anchors on a near-empty Concept.

**To do.** By rule: a short form that is a prefix of exactly one speaker's name in the same conversation (or one person Concept seen in the same session), used in an address or third-person reference, becomes an alt label (SKOS `altLabel`, B472 labels) of that person, not its own Concept. Ambiguous prefixes stay separate. Measure: aliases on the gate stores, and subject resolution on the questions that use one.
