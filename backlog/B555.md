# B555: turns that name an entity yield no fact about it

**Status:** Open. Found in the B554 design screen (2026-11-06).

**Principles:** P8 (subjects are the backbone; capture writes the edge), P1.

**What was seen.** On the R105 store copy (fresh ingest of LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50, B544 admission on), 80 of the 156 admitted named entities have no OBSERVATION_ABOUT edge. The B554 rule links an Observation to the entities its quote or clause names, and it reaches 32 of them. Of the 48 still unlinked:
- 24 have no Observation at all in any turn that names them: the extractor wrote no fact for those turns.
- About 11 are real entities (The Great Gatsby, Spain, the Grand Canyon, named people) where the extractor quoted a different sentence of the turn.

A span rule cannot fix either case, because the fact itself is missing.

**Measured (B543, R105 store copy).** The Loop is not the cause; extraction is. The 24 entities have no Observation in any turn that names them, across 26 turns. Observation extraction is a separate worker whose prompt is only the turn text, speaker and date, and the turn is enqueued whatever the Loop decided (B543 also stops a Loop failure from dropping it). Per turn, from the Observation worker's activity log: the model proposed nothing in 13, replied malformed in 3, and proposed claims that were all rejected in 10 (rejections: `bad_predicate` 15, `malformed_proposal` 5, `subject_not_grounded` 3, `quote_not_found` 2). No turn was skipped for being short. 23 of the 24 entities were withheld by Loop steps 2/4 in every naming turn, but that is the base rate (97% of admitted-entity occurrences are withheld), so it explains nothing, and B543 gains none of the 24. The model proposes nothing for 42% of all turns.

**To do (design first, extraction only).** Look at why the extractor writes no fact for a turn that names an entity: (1) the closed predicate set: `bad_predicate` is 15 of the 25 rejections (read which sentences they were and whether a fitting predicate exists among the nine); (2) the 3 malformed replies (retry or repair); (3) the 13 turns with no proposal: the closed question asked per entity, "what does this turn say about <entity>?", answered from the closed lists (B542 shape). A rule decides what is written (P3). Report how many of the 24 entities gain a fact, and whether the new facts are right (seeded hand audit).
