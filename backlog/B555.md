# B555: turns that name an entity yield no fact about it

**Status:** Open. Found in the B554 design screen (2026-11-06).

**Principles:** P8 (subjects are the backbone; capture writes the edge), P1.

**What was seen.** On the R105 store copy (fresh ingest of LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50, B544 admission on), 80 of the 156 admitted named entities have no OBSERVATION_ABOUT edge. The B554 rule links an Observation to the entities its quote or clause names, and it reaches 32 of them. Of the 48 still unlinked:
- 24 have no Observation at all in any turn that names them: the extractor wrote no fact for those turns.
- About 11 are real entities (The Great Gatsby, Spain, the Grand Canyon, named people) where the extractor quoted a different sentence of the turn.

A span rule cannot fix either case, because the fact itself is missing.

**To do (design first).** Measure on the store copy which turns naming an admitted entity produce no Observation, or produce none whose quote covers the entity, and why: the model skipped the sentence, or Loop steps 2/4 dropped the turn before extraction. A legacy-gate drop belongs to B543. Then decide the structural fix. Whether extraction reads every turn is B542's question, and the model reads every turn, so look at the closed question asked per entity: "what does this turn say about <entity>?". A rule decides what is written (P3). Report how many of the 35 entities gain a fact.
