# B470 - Common Query Words Must Not Double-Count Every Vector Hit in the Conversation Stage

State: complete (filed and fixed 2026-10-06; benchmark-confirmed 2026-10-07)
Priority: P2

### Problem Statement

`GraphGateway._bundle_conversation` (B454) fuses two lists by reciprocal rank,
adding 1/(60+rank) per list: vector hits (similarity >= 0.30) and FTS hits
(an OR of the query's content words, ranked by bm25). A query word that
most Messages contain puts nearly every Message in the FTS list. In a chat
history that word is a speaker's name: "Where has Melanie camped?" against
LoCoMo-10 conv-26, where the names are in ~80% of the 419 turns. Nearly every
vector hit then gets a second RRF term, and a turn found only by FTS can't
outscore them, even with its rare word at FTS rank 0. That turn is the
typical evidence turn, with a similarity just under the floor.

Measured with campy-benchmarks, on llama3.1:8b, gemma4:26b as judge, and
all-MiniLM-L6-v2:
- R8: median evidence rank by similarity is 28; the stage keeps 6.
- R9: raising the limit to 12 or 20 bought evidence recall but no accuracy
  that survives noise. It also halved adversarial abstention and doubled
  `ask` latency.
- R11 (`diag_locomo10_fusion.py`, an offline replay of the stage on the R2
  store, 46 questions):
  - plain FTS alone already beat the fused stage at limit 6;
  - dropping FTS query terms that occur in more than 20% of Messages raised
    the stage's evidence recall at limit 6 from 0.319 to 0.377 (21 → 23
    questions). No question lost its evidence.

### What It Does

- The FTS query leaves out every single-word query chunk in more than 20% of
  the Messages (`_COMMON_TERM_SHARE`). It also drops a possessive form such as
  "Melanie's".
  - Multi-part chunks (ids such as `memgym_path_ep3`) are never dropped.
  - If every content word is common, the query stays whole.
  - It applies only when the store holds at least 50 Messages
    (`_COMMON_TERM_MIN_DOCS`), where a share means something.
- The lexical-only filter (B459: two query content words, or the anchor word)
  still uses every query term, as the replay did.
- New `VectorStore.count_documents(prefixes)`.

### Not changed

- The vector channel, the 0.30 floor, the limit (6), RRF's k (60).
- Small stores (< 50 Messages): every campy-benchmarks fixture suite.

### Files

- `campy/brain/hippocampus/graph/gateway.py`: `_bundle_conversation`,
  `_lexical_query`, the constants.
- `campy/brain/hippocampus/graph/vector_store.py`: `count_documents`.
- `tests/test_b470_common_query_terms.py`.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [x] With 60+ Messages naming a speaker, an evidence turn found only through
      its rare word reaches the bundle over vector hits that share only the
      name (fails on main).
- [x] Under 50 Messages the query is unchanged.
- [x] Common single words and possessives are dropped; multi-part ids are
      kept; an all-common query stays whole.
- [x] Full suite: the same failure set as main (10 failures, all
      pre-existing), plus 4 passing tests.
- [x] campy-benchmarks LoCoMo-10 conv-26, 60 questions, on a real daemon:
      3 runs on each side (R13b + R15, llama3.1:8b, judge gemma4:26b,
      published in campy-benchmarks RESULTS.md):
      - judge accuracy: before (98ec223) 0.354 / 0.354 / 0.333, mean 0.347;
        after (71041ab) 0.396 / 0.375 / 0.396, mean **0.389**. The ranges
        don't overlap.
      - evidence recall 0.397 -> 0.411 in every run (retrieval is
        deterministic).
      - Questions passing in all 3 runs: 17 -> 19. q2 and q21 went 0/3 ->
        3/3; q6 went 2/3 -> 0/3.
