# B554: admitted entities get no fact, because the link reads the claim's words and not the source turn

**Status:** Implemented behind `[observations] evidence_entity_links` (env `CAMPY_EVIDENCE_ENTITY_LINKS`, default off; member of the `principled` profile). Offline receipt below. No GPU gate on its own; gated in the whole principled-profile gate. Found in R105 (B544 checkpoint, 2026-11-06).

**Principles:** P8 (subjects are the backbone: a missing edge is a capture fix), P3 (evidence stays linked to its source turn).

**What was seen.** With B544 entity admission on (R105, fresh ingest of unseen LoCoMo conv-26/41/48/49/50), 156 Concepts got an NER label. But 80 of them have no OBSERVATION_ABOUT link at all (43 of the 84 that are not people), for example The Great Gatsby, Paris, Fenway Park, Miami and Detroit. B544 links an Observation to an admitted entity only when the entity's name appears in the model's claim words (subject / object / event text). The model often paraphrases the name away ("prefers: really good" for a turn naming The Name of the Wind). So the entity is in the graph, but no fact hangs off it, and the pooled score did not move (0.503 -> 0.503).

**To do (design first).** Link by rule over the source text: an Observation is about an entity the turn admitted when that entity's span lies inside the Observation's evidence in its source turn. That evidence is the quote, or the clause or sentence holding it, decided by the dependency parse rather than by a word window. The model's wording plays no part. Measure on the R105 store copy: how many of the 80 entities gain a fact, and how many links are wrong (a sample checked by hand). The fact-to-entity link is a graph edge written at capture, and retrieval reads it through the existing OBSERVATION_ABOUT query.

**Related.**
- B552: evidence offsets do not select their quote, so match on the quote's text, or fix the offsets first.
- B549: NER label noise. R105 also shows Love, Nature and Music as WORK_OF_ART/ORG, and Karlie and Mustang as GPE; extend B549 beyond PERSON.
- B544 (#387).

## Implemented

Rule, over the source turn only (`temporal_lobe/observations/evidence_entities.py`): the quote is located in the turn text (`typed_objects.locate_quote`); an admitted entity whose occurrence (spaCy span, or the same text exactly as written) lies inside the quote links (tier 1); an occurrence outside the quote in the same clause links (tier 2, clause by dependency parse); an entity a quote of the turn holds is not tier-2-linked to another Observation; a list mate (coordination or apposition) of an entity inside the quote, a form of address, and a speaker outside the quote do not link. Edge: the existing `OBSERVATION_ABOUT` (no marker). Coverage read: `entity_admission.unlinked_entities`. Backfill: `scripts/backfill_evidence_links.py` (dry run by default).

Offline receipt (fresh copy of the R105 store, 2311 live Observations, backfill dry run): 208 links planned, 89 new, 30 of the 80 unlinked entities gain a fact (GPE 10, PERSON 10, ORG 6, LOC 2, PRODUCT 1, WORK_OF_ART 1). Design-time measure before the list-mate fix and on all 2316 Observations: 221 / 95 new / 32 of 80; hand sample 25/30 right (4 wrong were tagger noise in the entity itself, B549; 1 was the list sibling this fix removes). Entities still missed: 24 are named in no turn that has an Observation (extractor gap, separate card), the rest are named in a sentence no quote or clause reaches.
