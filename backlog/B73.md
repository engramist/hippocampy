### B73 · Batch Outcome Signal Lookups in current_truth

**Problem:** For each Concept returned by vector search in `current_truth`, a separate `db.execute()` queries `OUTCOME_SIGNAL` edges. With 10 concepts across 8 tables, worst case = 80 sequential queries — a classic N+1 problem.

**What it does:** Replaces per-concept OUTCOME_SIGNAL lookups with a single batched `UNWIND` query.

**Depends on:** None

**Files to modify:**
- `mcp_engine/tools/__init__.py` — refactor outcome signal lookup to batch query

**Adapter allow-list propagation check:** No tool changes — N/A.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- `current_truth` makes at most 1 OUTCOME_SIGNAL query regardless of result count (not N queries)
- Outcome boost values are identical before and after the change (behavioral equivalence)
- `pytest tests/ -k current_truth -q` passes
- Performance improvement measurable on graphs with >50 concepts

**Outcome:** `current_truth` scales linearly with graph size instead of quadratically with result count.
