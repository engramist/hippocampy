# B401 - Measure the Value of the spaCy Step Before Replacing It

State: blocked (needs B400 merged)
Priority: P1
Branch: feat/b401-extraction-value-measurement

Design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-entity-candidate-generation-design.md

> **Read the design doc first.** This card is deliberately thin because the reasoning
> lives there. Running these three queries without understanding *why* they decide the
> design will produce numbers nobody can act on.

### Problem

B387 measured an ONNX NER replacement at F1 0.534 against spaCy. That number compares
two candidate generators and cannot say which is *correct* — spaCy is the incumbent,
not ground truth, and neither was scored against labelled data.

The design doc argues the framing is wrong: spaCy's only load-bearing output is
**candidate spans** (its labels are discarded, its relations backstopped by Step 3b's
LLM), while the graph already carries a SKOS `Label` layer — a growing gazetteer of
every entity ever committed, with aliases — that Step 1 never consults.

> **Note on gazetteer data (B405 fix):** Prior to B405, concept deduplication aborted mid-merge
> due to a schema mismatch (`created_at` written to `HAS_ALT_LABEL`, which has no properties).
> As a result, no `HAS_ALT_LABEL` edges were successfully created in production prior to B405,
> and the gazetteer contained zero alt-labels. Measurement (1) must take this into account.

Before building any replacement, measure whether the step earns its place.

### The three measurements

Replay the existing graph. No new system is built in this card.

1. **Gazetteer coverage.** What fraction of spaCy-proposed spans already exist as a
   `Label` (pref or alt) in the graph?
2. **Origin split.** What fraction of committed `Concept` nodes originated from spaCy
   NER versus the `doc.noun_chunks` fallback? (`step1_ner.py`'s docstring implies the
   fallback dominates — confirm or refute.)
3. **Step 1b survival.** What fraction of Step 1b dependency-parse relations survive
   filtering, versus being superseded by Step 3b's LLM `extract_semantic_relations()`?

Report each with the query used and the sample size, so the numbers can be reproduced.

### Decision rule — fixed in advance, do not renegotiate after seeing results

- (1) high **and** (3) low → **delete the spaCy step**; link against the graph instead.
- (1) high, (3) high → keep a parser, demote NER behind a gazetteer tier.
- (1) low → gazetteer not yet dense enough; keep spaCy, revisit after more ingestion.

Committing before measuring is intentional. If the numbers land somewhere the rule
does not cleanly cover, **report that and escalate** rather than picking the nearest
branch.

### Explicitly NOT in scope

- Do **not** implement gazetteer matching, Aho-Corasick, or any replacement pipeline.
- Do **not** change ingestion behaviour.

This card produces **numbers and a recommendation**, nothing else.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All three fractions measured against the real graph, with queries and sample sizes shown.
- [ ] Result mapped to the decision rule, or escalated if it does not fit.
- [ ] Findings appended to the design doc so the reasoning and the evidence live together.
- [ ] Zero production behaviour changes in this card.

### Outcome

The extractor decision is made on evidence about what the graph actually needs, rather
than on agreement between two candidate generators.
