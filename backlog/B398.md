# B398 - Patent Evidence Re-Certification After Ingestion-Plane Swap

State: blocked (needs B387 merged)
Priority: P0
Branch: feat/b398-patent-recert-ingestion
PR Target: main

### Problem

B380 froze zero-mock verification for 9 patent claims against the current codebase,
ahead of the March 25 2027 non-provisional filing deadline. The plan budgeted a
**dual-engine re-certification at the storage cutover** (B397) — but nothing
re-certifies the evidence after the **ingestion plane** changes.

B387 replaces spaCy NER and the dependency-parse-derived features with ONNX. Three
claims describe mechanisms that run through exactly that path:

- **Claim 1 — Gated Consolidation Loop:** Steps 1 and 1b are the entity and relation
  extraction B387 rewrites.
- **Claim 2 — Shape-First Principle:** ontological grounding operates on the entity
  spans and types Step 1 produces.
- **Claim 4 — Cocktail Party Selective Attention Gate:** the salience thresholds
  (<0.60 noise, >0.90 salient) are calibrated against embeddings of extracted spans.

If extraction changes shape, the evidence pack describes a mechanism that no longer
matches the shipped code. That is precisely the failure mode B380 exists to prevent,
and it is the kind of gap patent counsel finds rather than we do.

### Scope

1. Re-run the full `tests/patent_claims/` suite against the post-B387 ingestion
   plane and record traces.
2. **Quantify extraction-behaviour delta** for Claims 1, 2, 4: entity span
   agreement, type-assignment agreement, and downstream salience-score distribution
   vs. the pre-B387 spaCy baseline captured at B380.
3. Regenerate `docs/patent-evidence-pack.md` with post-swap traces, keeping the
   pre-swap traces as a dated appendix. The evidence pack should show the mechanism
   holds across implementations — that is a **stronger** filing position than a
   single-implementation snapshot, the same argument as the dual-engine story.
4. Where a claim's language is tied to a spaCy-specific artifact (dependency labels,
   noun chunks) rather than the mechanism itself, **flag it for patent counsel**.
   Do not rewrite claim language unilaterally.

### Escalation rule

If any claim test's behaviour changes materially, **stop and escalate.** Do not
adjust assertions or thresholds to make the suite green. A claim whose evidence
requires a moved threshold is a claim whose scope changed, and that is a legal
question, not an engineering one.
