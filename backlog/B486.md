# B486 - Read-Time Quality Filter for Observations

State: implemented (off by default; gate pending: LoCoMo replay with `quality_filter` on vs off)
Priority: P2

Plan: `learnings/2026-10-m3-observations.md` ("Extraction precision is the next lever"); `backlog/B472.md`

### Problem Statement

About half of the extracted Observations are low quality: pronoun or generic
objects ("I has_attribute people"), second-person remarks filed as the
speaker's own ("It must have been special at your wedding" -> `I did wedding`),
greetings ("Enjoy your day!"), bare verbs ("I did make"). They take slots in
the semantic section's `observation_limit` (8) that specific facts could fill.

Per the graph principle (tag at capture, filter at query time), nothing is
dropped at capture. The reader scores and skips.

### Fix

- `campy/brain/hippocampus/observation_quality.py`: pure, stdlib-only
  `observation_quality(obs) -> (score in [0,1], reasons)`. General rules, no
  dataset special-casing; the module docstring lists them with their penalties
  (`PENALTIES`). Uses the existing FTS stopword list.
- `[observations] quality_filter = false` (env `CAMPY_OBSERVATIONS_QUALITY_FILTER`)
  and `quality_min = 0.7`. With the flag on, `_stage_observations` fetches
  `min(5 * observation_limit, 64)` candidates through the new NamedQuery
  `thalamus.bundle_observations_pool` (same handler, larger `limit`; the
  existing query is untouched), drops those under `quality_min`, then cuts to
  `observation_limit`, so better candidates backfill. If none pass, the stage
  returns None and the old semantic section is used. Flag off: byte-identical.

### Calibration (offline, no LLM)

Observations dumped from copies of the golden stores r44-conv-42 and r58
(1034 live). 60 labelled by hand as good (a specific, true-looking fact about
the speaker or a named person, supported by the quote) or junk, rules tuned on
those; then a fresh 40 labelled with the rules frozen. At `quality_min` 0.7:

| Sample | Base precision | Kept | Precision | Recall of good | Junk removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dev 60 (27 good) | 0.45 | 31 | 0.77 | 0.89 | 26 / 33 |
| Held-out 40 (15 good) | 0.38 | 18 | 0.72 | 0.87 | 20 / 25 |

Labels are the author's single-rater judgment; the held-out sample is the
honest number. The effect on answer accuracy is not measured.
