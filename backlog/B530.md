# B530: negation and hypothetical scope by a standard library and algorithm

**Status:** Open

Capture rules hand-code negation and irrealis cues (B496, B523's `irrealis`, the extractor's polarity). The NLP-standards survey found one maintained drop-in that fits our stack: negspacy (MIT, spaCy ≥3.8). It also found a 15-year-evaluated algorithm for the rest: ConText (trigger, direction, scope, terminator) for hypothetical, reported and future scope. The rule from that survey is to map first, then define.

**Work:**
1. Trial negspacy in place of our hand negation cues on a store copy. Report the cue decisions that change.
2. Re-express the irrealis and hypothetical guards as ConText-style rules on spaCy's SpanRuler and DependencyMatcher (medspaCy only if it installs cleanly).
3. Gate on the holdout. Success means fewer hand-written scope functions with no score loss.
