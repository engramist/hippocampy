## Patent Center Submission Order

1. Log in to Patent Center.
2. Start a new filing and choose `Provisional Application`.
3. Upload the specification first: `InvertorsDocs/PPA-Specification-Draft.docx`.
4. Upload the drawings next as a single PDF: `InvertorsDocs/PPA-Figures-Labeled-Combined.pdf`.
5. Upload the provisional cover sheet: `InvertorsDocs/sb0016-filled-flat.pdf`.
6. Upload the micro-entity certification: `InvertorsDocs/sb0015a-filled-flat.pdf`.
7. Review the generated document list inside Patent Center and make sure only the intended packet documents are attached.
8. Confirm micro-entity status inside Patent Center matches the uploaded certification.
9. Review fee screens and confirm the provisional filing fee amount before payment.
10. Submit and save the filing receipt, application number, and filing date immediately.
